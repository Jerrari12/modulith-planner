# Validation scripts

Run everything from the `parts-drawer-generator` folder (the one holding `site/` and `validation/`). The scripts read the reference meshes from the sibling folders `../generator-assets/` (shells, holder stamps, Fusion reference internals, the Small label) and `../large-label-test/` (the Large label), and write their output to `out/`.

Requirements: Node 20+, Python 3.11+ with `manifold3d trimesh rtree scipy numpy`. Browser tests: `playwright` for Python with Chromium.

## Geometry (Node)

| Script | Checks |
|---|---|
| `node validation/t_floor.mjs` | The shipped 5×4 through the v2 model (at all four heights), option variants (trays up to 12 mm, Deep scoops), and 160 random layouts with random scoops and tray sizes, each at a random size and height (v24 lengths, v25 widths). Every build must be one solid. Writes `out/floor/` and `out/floorfuzz/`. |
| `node validation/t_walls.mjs` | 150 random editing sessions (wall moves, whole-line moves, merges, splits, typed sizes) through the same functions the page uses, each at a random size and height (v24 lengths, v25 widths). Writes `out/walls/` (STL + layout JSON). |
| `node validation/t_inv.mjs` | Parts-list parsing, Part Picker items, and every kit at all four heights and both label sizes: tiling, holders, fullness. |
| `node validation/t_color.mjs` | Floor-color parts: body + floor parts equal the plain drawer's volume, zero overlap. Writes `out/color/`. |
| `node validation/t_export.mjs` | The 3MF and STL writers. Needs JSZip: `npm i jszip@3.10.1` in this folder, or `NPM_DIR` as for the browser tests. |
| `node validation/t_marks.mjs` | Part outlines and the bottom-marked layout code on five kit drawers, plain and colored. Writes `out/marks/`. |
| `node validation/t_lid.mjs` | The lid on 51 layouts (7 to 10 column grids, 1.5H and 2H included): one solid, no overlap with the drawer seated or nudged 0.29 mm, rests on the dividers, top 2.2 under the rim and at least 0.4 under the drawer stoppers' tabs (`STOPPER` in gen.js), lifted as far as a case allows (2.48) every lip still reaches 1 mm below the divider tops, and every lid has a working fingertip pull with a 1.2 hook. Writes `out/lid/` (lids, print-oriented lids, test pieces). |
| `node validation/t_covers.mjs` | Compartment covers on 34 layouts (10×8, a 12 mm grid, 1.5H and 2H included): each cover one solid, no overlap with the drawer seated or nudged 0.29 mm, rests on the dividers, meets its beads within 0.4 mm of lift, has a working 6 mm nail slot under a 1.2 panel, its top at least 0.4 under the stopper tabs, no two covers overlap, the drawer with beads stays one solid, and the named grids get the expected number of covers. Since v26 it also checks that a cover over a label holder leaves the label open (nothing over it beyond 1.0 at its front edge, and its right end beside a leg) and gets its L leg exactly where the holder's side is open, at least 4 wide. Also checks the test piece against 0.5 mm beads. Writes `out/covers/`. |
| `validation/t_lid_pull.mjs` | Shared by the two above: a pull works when a straight path down through its opening is clear, its hook space (both sides for a nail slot) is empty and the top over it is solid (up to the chamfer, for a nail slot), its chamfered top edge is there, and it is at least 20 × 9 with a 2.5 hook (finger) or 9 × 6 with 1.5 each side (nail). |
| `node validation/slot_sec.mjs` then `python validation/plot_slot.py` | The section drawing of a cover's nail slot and both cover sizes from above (`out/slot/cover-nail-slot.png`). Python needs `shapely` for trimesh's sections. |
| `node validation/slot_material.mjs` | How much material surrounds each cover's nail slot on 28 layouts: plate from the cover's front edge to the slot and the divot, the cup's room to the skirt, and the cup end wall left where a side bead's window cuts it. Prints the least of each. |
| `node validation/cover_test_prints.mjs [dir]` | Writes the cover test set to `cover-print-files/test-pieces/` (or `dir`): a 0.5H middle compartment, the 0.5H front-left corner (outer-wall snaps), the middle compartment at 1H, a 10×8 cell (9 mm slot) and (v26) a 52 mm compartment with its L cover, each with its cover, plus the corner compartment with 0.5 mm beads. |
| `node validation/cover_snaps.mjs` | How many snaps covers get over every uniform grid the page makes and 300 random merged layouts (none may have fewer than two). |
| `node validation/t_cells.mjs` | Cells: bearing wells (v20, and v19 `posts` opening as wells), nozzle and bit sockets roomy and dense, and washer rows (v19) on the shipped 5×4 at all four heights and both label sizes, open, with the lid and with covers, plus merged blocks open, lidded and dense, and coloured floors. Every drawer one solid, every cell and row 2 mm inside its open floor, the spacing rules (8 mm roomy, 5 dense, half to the edge, wells' gap at their notches), each well clear of its bearing stack with notches narrower than the bearing, nothing held over the height limit (the lid's ribs and, for washers, its lips included), each washer seated on both flanks of its groove, nothing a cell holds meeting the lid or a cover, the lid pull never over a cell, and coloured floors going round the cells (body one solid, parts adding up exactly, no colour under washer rows). Writes `out/cells_0.5H.stl`, `out/cells_1H.stl` and `out/cells_washers_0.5H.stl`. |
| `node validation/t_heights.mjs` | The tall shells (v23; the 2H groove v25), on the generator's own shells: the stretch of the 1H by 28 and 56 mm is one clean solid with its rim at 78 / 106, its band section the 1H's at three heights, below the band the 1H exactly, above it the 1H moved up, volume the 1H's plus section × stretch, and equal to the v23 shell file; the 1.5H shell is exactly that stretch; the 2H shell is the stretch plus material on its groove floors only, with the Decor 240-2W-2H's groove outline. A shipped 5×4 at both tall heights and label sizes keeps the 1H's top 20 mm (holders, divider tops, rim) and floor exactly (at 2H inside the groove floors). |
| `node validation/t_sizes.mjs` | Every size: the lengths (v24) at 1W to 4W (v25). Widths first: the 1W, 3W and 4W made from the 2W at 0.5H and 1H (`widthShell`) are one clean solid, 88 × W − 13 wide, their ends exactly the 2W's and each 88 mm period the 2W's moved over, their volume the 2W's plus a period per added width, with no vertex in the stretch bands. Then for all 84 size and height shells (`shellFor`): one clean solid, 88 × W − 13 wide outside with 4.3 in front of the cavity and 4 behind, rim where `SIZES` says; the front 15 mm exactly the same width's 240 and the back that 240's moved by the length difference; a 1 mm slice in the straight band equal to that 240's; volume that 240's plus that slice times the difference; the 240-2W 1.5H equal to the v23 shell and the 2H equal to it inside its groove floors (it reads `shell_240-2W-15H.stl` and `-2H.stl`). Per size and height, the default grid builds as one solid. At 1.5H and 2H, the reachable grid passes the reach rule, and one more column or row would not. Every kit laid out there is reachable or reports its capacity. Also checks layouts moving between sizes (`rescaleDepth`, `rescaleWidth`), layout codes carrying the size (240-2W codes unchanged), the lid and covers at eight sizes from 59-1W to 240-4W, and the bottom mark at 59-1W, 59-2W, 240-1W and 270-4W. |
| `python validation/decor_to_stl.py <raw GLB folder> out/decor` then `node validation/t_widths_decor.mjs out/decor` | The shell construction against Joey's own Decor drawers (v25). The first turns his Decor drawer GLB exports (on D: under `decor-drawers-v2609-2026-10-19\previous (July 2026 export)\<L>\raw`; copy the sizes you want into one folder) into STLs in the drawer frame. The second builds each of them from his Decor 240-2W exactly as `shellFor` builds a parts drawer and compares: every 0.5H and 1H size (240 at 1W, 3W and 4W; 59 to 270 at 2W; 59-1W, 115-3W, 270-4W) within 0.05 mm³ each way. His 1.5H and 2H differ by Decor-only features and the 2H groove; it lists those. |
| `node validation/cell_test_prints.mjs [dir]` | Writes the v20 cell test prints to `cell-print-files/test-pieces/` (or `dir`): bearing wells and washer rows in compartments cut from a 0.5H drawer, and 3 × 3 nozzle and bit socket grids on plates. |
| `node validation/t_kit.mjs` | Kit drawers (v18): the Voron 2.4 LDO Rev D list (from `kit-drawers/`) parses line for line, every part is placed exactly once three ways (mixed heights, all 0.5H, all 1H), every drawer tiles and stays under its fill limit, and old fastener lines parse as before. |

## Checks on the output (Python)

| Script | Checks |
|---|---|
| `python validation/check_against_fusion.py out/floor/shipped_05H.stl 05H` (and `shipped_1H.stl 1H`) | Max surface deviation from the Fusion part. Expect 0.0001 mm. |
| `python validation/chk_mesh.py 'out/floorfuzz/*.stl' 'out/walls/*.stl' ...` | Welds at 1e-4 mm like a slicer, then counts non-manifold edges and bodies. Use it on drawer and body STLs; floor-color part files are several separate tiles by design. |
| `python validation/label_sweep.py` (after `t_walls.mjs`) | 28 holders from the wall-edit layouts (end-fill, wide, narrow): label seat, slide-in and lift against the shipped values. |
| `python validation/label_tray.py` (after `t_floor.mjs`) | The same label checks with 12 mm trays and Deep scoops. |
| `python validation/gcode_tools.py file.gcode` | Tool changes and M600 positions in a sliced file (PrusaSlicer 2.7 CLI). |
| `validation/refgen.py` | The Python reference implementation of the first generator. |

## Browser (Playwright)

`pwfloor.py` is the harness: it wraps `site/index.html` in a page skeleton, serves `site/` on 127.0.0.1, and routes the three.js and JSZip CDN URLs to local copies. Put `npm pack three@0.170.0` and `npm pack jszip@3.10.1` extracted as `three/package` and `jszip/package` in a folder and point `NPM_DIR` at it (default `../npm`). Then run `python validation/pwphase.py` (tray sizes, 3D select and paint, outlines, layout codes, export), `pwlid.py` (the lid), `pwcovers.py` (compartment covers), `pwpulls.py` (nail slots on every grid, the lid's pull on 10×8), `pwwalls.py`, `pwinv.py`, `pwcolor.py`, `pwheights.py` (v23: height buttons and hint, the reach note, a 2H export and share text, parts-list height tips, lid and covers at the tall heights), `pwsizes.py` (v24, widths v25: the collection and width buttons, the footprint line, a layout moving to another length or width or starting over with its note, the 59 at 0.5H and 1H and at 1W and 2W only, exports at 115-2W and 240-3W, share texts reopening at 185-1.5H and 240-3W, columns up to 5 per width unit, the reach warning with its count, plan hatching, legend and fix (2 × 3 at 240-2H, 3 × 5 at 1.5H, 2 × 4 at 270-2H, 5 × 3 at 240-4W-2H), merges against the warning, a parts list too long for a 2H drawer, the lid at 59 and covers at 270 and 270-4W, both button rows on one line at phone width; `pwpulls.py` is slow in software rendering, so run it on its own), `pwcells.py` (cells from the selection panel, the bearing and washer pickers, roomy and dense spacing, washer rows under the lid and with a washer part, floor colours, share text and a v19 share text with posts, the parts list leaving kit parts out) or `pwfloor.py` itself (scoops, trays, merge and split, export, the phone layout).

`site/index.html` is a page body: the artifact host adds the doctype and head. To open it locally, use the harness or add a document skeleton.
